About

Meet Daniel Obute

A digital illustrator crafting vivid, story-rich characters for comics, animation, games, and NFT projects.

My Journey

That moment changed everything.

What started as pure passion became something more when Daniel was first approached for a commissioned piece.

That moment changed everything.

Since then, he has been building his craft, working with collectors, and developing a style that blends strong personality, detailed execution, and storytelling.

Key Milestones

Building in public, across continents.

Bored Ape community

Creator of Evolution 30 for the Bored Ape community

Exhibitions

Exhibited artwork at multiple events in the United States and Africa

Collectors worldwide

Collected and printed by fans worldwide

Process

Every character starts with gesture and pose.

Daniel focuses on capturing energy first, how a character feels, before refining the details. This approach gives his work a dynamic, expressive style that stands out.

Philosophy

Daniel’s goal is simple:

To create joy and inspire others to keep going, keep creating, and believe in their path.

Trust the process

Looking Ahead

Beyond the canvas.

Daniel is building toward a future where his characters exist beyond the canvas.

He aims to collaborate with animation studios like Netflix, Disney, DreamWorks, and Sony and to see his creations brought to life on screen.

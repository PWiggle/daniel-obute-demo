Style

Stylised design with cinematic storytelling.

Blending stylised character design with cinematic storytelling, drawing inspiration from modern animation, comic culture, and supernatural themes. Influenced by productions like Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse and Sony’s hybrid 2D/3D animation style, his art combines expressive poses, playful energy, and emotional depth with bold lighting and dynamic form.

Inspired by artists like Ben Eblen and Loish, Daniel creates characters that feel vibrant, slightly surreal, and full of personality. Balancing clean stylisation with atmosphere, movement, and narrative presence.

About

Meet Daniel Obute

Daniel is a digital illustrator driven by a lifelong passion for storytelling and character creation.

Inspired by cartoons, real-life experiences, and music, he creates stylised characters that are expressive, dynamic, and full of personality.

Through his art, Daniel aims to create joy and inspiration, encouraging others to keep creating and trust their journey.
